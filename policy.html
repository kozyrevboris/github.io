Policy Work

Alongside my academic research, I contribute to policy-oriented work on macroeconomic developments and the economic outlook. This work draws on empirical evidence, professional expertise, and modern forecasting methods to produce forecasts for the global economy, the euro area, and the German economy for the current and following year. Specifically, since autumn 2021, I have been contributing to forecasts of gross value added.

The links below present reports to which I have contributed since 2021: the quarterly IWH Macroeconomic Reports and the Joint Economic Forecasts, published twice a year in spring and autumn. The final reports are in German. If you are interested in any of the results or would like further information, please feel free to email me.
